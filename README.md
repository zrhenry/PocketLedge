##  The Idea Behind PocketLedger

The idea for this project came to me when my father was managing the financial expenses of a house we were building. He had to spend a lot of time entering expenses and financial records into Excel spreadsheets.

I realized that a simple Python program could make this process much faster and easier.

PocketLedger is designed to help users record financial transactions quickly, organize them by date and type, and easily access the information they need. For example, users can track the cost of construction materials such as iron, review expenses on a specific date, and calculate their income and expenses without manually searching through Excel spreadsheets.

My goal is to make financial record-keeping simpler, faster, and more accessible, while providing clear reports that help users understand where their money is going.
